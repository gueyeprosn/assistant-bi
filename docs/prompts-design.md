## 1. Prompt Logo

```
Professional brand logo for "Assistant Bi", Senegalese B2B WhatsApp AI virtual secretary for small businesses in Dakar (salons, garages, nurses, artisans).

Name: Assistant Bi only. Do not write SecrétAIRE Local.

Style: Minimalism & Swiss Style, flat vector, 2 colors, high contrast, geometric, essential. Logo brief: Classic Blue Trust + Navy Professional. Mark suggests a calm secretary + chat, no robot, no brain, no circuit, no infinity loop.

Colors strictly:
- Deep navy #0B1F3A
- Soft gold #C9A84C
- White #FFFFFF

Deliver on pure white:
1) Rounded-square app icon 1024px, navy field, gold+white mark
2) Horizontal lockup: icon + wordmark "Assistant Bi" in Source Sans 3 Bold
3) Reverse lockup white+gold on navy
4) 16px readability test

NOT: photorealistic, 3D, gradients, purple AI, baobab, lion, mask, kente, map of Africa, clip art, watermark.

Output: Figma-ready logo board, vector look, no clutter.
```

## 2. Prompt Charte graphique complète

```
Complete brand guidelines / graphic charter for "Assistant Bi", Senegalese B2B SaaS WhatsApp AI secretary. Audience: low digital literacy, low-end Android, slow 3G. Paid professional product. Light mode only.

UI UX Pro Max: Minimalism & Swiss Style, variance 3, motion 2, density 3 (spacious). Override any AI-purple recommendation.

Brand book 10 pages on white:
1. Name Assistant Bi + line "Votre secrétaire, déjà sur WhatsApp."
2. Logo system, clear space, min 24px, do/don't
3. Colors with HEX: navy #0B1F3A, navy-2 #16345C, gold #C9A84C, gold-2 #D4B96A, white #FFFFFF, ink #0B1F3A, muted #4A5A6D, line #D7DEE8, soft #F4F6F9. Forbidden: #7C3AED purple, WhatsApp green as brand.
4. Type: Source Sans 3 only, body 17px/1.45, headings 28–40 bold
5. Components: 48px tap, 12px radius, 1px borders, no shadows
6. Stroke icons 1.8px navy, no emoji icons
7. FR | WO toggle
8. 4-tab app nav: Accueil, Agenda, Messages, Plus
9. Voice: simple French + everyday Dakar Wolof
10. Anti-patterns: glassmorphism, dark mode, gradients, robots, African folklore clichés

Output: high-res brand book, design tokens ready, professional B2B African startup, not folkloric.
```
